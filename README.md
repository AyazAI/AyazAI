## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm an AI engineer and full stack developer who builds intelligent products end to end. I started as a web and mobile developer, and over the past few years my work has shifted toward putting large language models to real use: chat assistants that know a company's data, agents that finish multi-step tasks on their own, and automation pipelines that quietly remove hours of manual work.

I like the whole journey. I'll prototype an idea in a Python notebook, turn it into a clean API, wrap it in a polished React interface, and ship it to the cloud with monitoring and evals so it keeps working after launch.

- 🤖 **AI systems:** LLM apps, RAG pipelines, tool-using agents, and fine-tuned models built with **Python, LangChain, LangGraph and PyTorch**
- 🧠 **Model providers:** hands-on with **Claude, OpenAI and open-source models** through Hugging Face and Ollama
- ⚙️ **Automation:** workflow agents and integrations that connect LLMs to CRMs, email, voice, and internal tools
- 🌐 **Product engineering:** production apps with **React, Next.js, Node.js, FastAPI and AWS**
- 📊 **Data and ML:** classic machine learning and computer vision with **scikit-learn, TensorFlow and OpenCV**
- 🎨 **Design:** I prototype in Figma so the AI feels natural to use, not bolted on
- 💬 **Ask me about:** building with LLMs, RAG, agents, Python, React, Next.js, Node.js, AWS, or anything you wish!
- 📫 **Reach me at:** [ayaz.uiit3@gmail.com](mailto:ayaz.uiit3@gmail.com)
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I am way more creative than you think!
